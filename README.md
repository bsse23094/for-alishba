# 🌸 For Alishba — The Never-Wilting Flower

> *"They said real flowers die in the mail. So here’s one that never will."*

A pure, fullscreen 3D interactive flower built specifically for **Alishba**. Zero clutter, zero extra distractions—just an aesthetic, mind-blowing 3D blooming rose with gorgeous particle atmosphere and sweet interactive touches.

---

## ✨ Features & Interactions

- 🌹 **Full-Screen 3D Procedural Flower**: Multi-layered blooming English rose with curved parametric petals, organic stem, and glowing stamen core.
- 🎛️ **Interactive Bloom Slider & Toggle**: Watch the petals smoothly unfurl in 3D from a tight bud into full bloom.
- 🎨 **5 Color Themes**:
  - 🌸 *Blush Peony* (Warm rose & champagne)
  - 💜 *Lavender Dream* (Lilac & violet glow)
  - 🌅 *Golden Sunset* (Coral, apricot & peach)
  - 🌙 *Midnight Mirage* (Deep indigo & cyan fairy glow)
  - 🤍 *Pure Silk* (Ivory pearl & gold)
- 💧 **Watering Effect**: Drops of glowing water cascade down, causing the flower to pulse with light and burst into bloom.
- 🌸 **Pluck Petal / Tap Flower**: Plucks a 3D petal that twirls down gracefully and reveals a sweet, giggle-inducing little thought about her.
- 🎵 **Cozy Lo-Fi Ambient Synthesizer**: Procedural Rhodes electric piano chords (Web Audio API, zero external MP3s needed).
- 🔄 **360° Controls**: Smooth drag to spin, pinch/scroll to zoom.
